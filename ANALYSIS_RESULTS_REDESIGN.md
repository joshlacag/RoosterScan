## 🎨 DESIGN SPECIFICATIONS

### Color Coding:
- **Healthy/Low Risk:** Green (#22c55e)
- **Medium Risk:** Yellow/Orange (#f59e0b)
- **High Risk:** Red (#ef4444)
- **Info:** Blue (#3b82f6)

### Typography:
- **Headers:** Bold, 24-32px
- **Body:** Regular, 14-16px
- **Metrics:** Bold, 18-24px
- **Labels:** Medium, 12-14px

### Spacing:
- **Section gaps:** 24-32px
- **Card padding:** 20-24px
- **Element spacing:** 12-16px

### Shadows & Borders:
- **Cards:** Subtle shadow (0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.1))
- **Borders:** 1-2px solid with brand color
- **Hover effects:** Slight elevation increase
