### **After (6 detailed tips + bonus):**
```
✓ Side view (Profile):
  Capture from the side at 90° angle. Shows all 17 keypoints 
  clearly (head, wings, legs, tail).

✓ Good lighting:
  Natural daylight or bright indoor lighting. Avoid shadows 
  on the rooster's body.

✓ Full body visible:
  Include entire rooster from beak to tail tip. Don't crop 
  out wings, legs, or head.

✓ Clear background:
  Plain, contrasting background (grass, concrete, or solid 
  color). Avoid clutter.

✓ Camera distance:
  Stand 3-5 feet (1-1.5 meters) away. Rooster should fill 
  60-80% of the frame.

✓ Rooster posture:
  Natural standing position. Wait for rooster to stand still 
  (not walking or jumping).

⚠️ Tip: Close-up images of specific areas (feet, wings, head) 
also work for detailed inspection!
```

## 💡 WHY THESE SPECIFIC TIPS?

### **1. Side View (90° angle)**
- **Reason:** All 17 keypoints visible from profile
- **Science:** Front/back views hide keypoints
- **Result:** Higher detection accuracy

### **2. Camera Distance (3-5 feet)**
- **Reason:** Optimal resolution for keypoint detection
- **Science:** Too close = distortion, too far = low detail
- **Result:** Balanced image quality

### **3. Rooster Should Fill 60-80% of Frame**
- **Reason:** Maximizes pixel density on rooster
- **Science:** More pixels = better feature extraction
- **Result:** Higher confidence scores

### **4. Natural Standing Position**
- **Reason:** Consistent pose for comparison
- **Science:** Walking/jumping creates motion blur
- **Result:** Clearer keypoint detection

### **5. Avoid Shadows**
- **Reason:** Shadows hide anatomical features
- **Science:** Computer vision struggles with high contrast
- **Result:** Better edge detection

### **6. Plain Background**
- **Reason:** Reduces visual noise
- **Science:** AI focuses on rooster, not background
- **Result:** Faster, more accurate detection
